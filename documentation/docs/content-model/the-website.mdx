# The Website

The Website section covers public-facing research pages and the TEHS Quarterly digital archive.

## Research Articles & Overviews

Use a **Research Article** for long-form modern research, overviews, or interactive pages—not for raw transcriptions of historical clippings.

Key fields:

- **Archive ID** (`archiveId`) — unique catalog number when matching an imported Book or legacy record
- **Page title** and required **URL slug** (generated from the title)
- **Townships** — geographic scope of the research article
- **Page content & layout canvas** — Portable Text with:
  - Text blocks and **Uploaded Images** (caption, alt, and semantic `imageRole`: figure / aside)
  - **Interactive map modules** (`mapYear`, embedded `mapUrl`)
  - **Nested navigation portals** linking to other research articles
- **Notes & References** (`notesAndReferences`) — published bibliographic notes, endnotes, and web links (rich text with URL marks). Appears with the article on the public site.
- **Internal Comments** (`internalComments`) — Studio-only working notes. **Will not appear on the public site.**

Related: [Publish a research article](../common-tasks/publish-research-article.mdx), [Set Archive IDs](../common-tasks/set-archive-ids.mdx)

## TEHS Quarterly Articles

A **TEHS Quarterly Article** stores the full digitized text of a Quarterly piece, plus publication metadata and entity links.

Key fields:

- **Publication Details** — title, author name, volume, issue, publication date (text), and start page for table-of-contents ordering
- **Article Content** — Portable Text body with:
  - Standard text blocks (paragraphs, headings)
  - Inline images (caption and alt text)
  - **Original Print Page Break** blocks to preserve academic pagination (e.g., "Page 3") without disrupting reading flow
  - **Notes & References** (`notesAndReferences`) — published bibliographic notes, endnotes, and web links (rich text with URL marks). Appears with the article on the public site.
- **Tagged Entities** — references to properties and people mentioned in the article
- **Internal Comments** (`internalComments`) — Studio-only working notes. **Will not appear on the public site.**

Archive documents (primary sources and historical images) link these under **Research References** (`citations`). That field is a document reference, not a pasted URL: many archive items can share one article. Historical images also have a separate **References** rich-text field for books, PDFs, and other links that are not Quarterly articles in this Studio.

**Notes & References** on a Research Article or Quarterly Article is bibliography *on the article itself*. It is not the same as archive **Research References** (document links *to* a Quarterly article) or image **References** (books, PDFs, and other URLs on a Historical Image).

### Dynamic table of contents

Because articles are structured data rather than static HTML, the website can generate volume indexes on the fly. For example, to recreate the Volume 15 table of contents:

```groq
*[_type == "quarterlyArticle" && volume == 15] | order(startPage asc) {
  title,
  authorText,
  startPage,
  publishedDate
}
```
