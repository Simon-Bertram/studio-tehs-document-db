# The Website

The Website section covers public-facing research pages, Then & Now features, the TEHS Quarterly digital archive, and the menus on the public Astro site.

## Public site navigation

One singleton document holds the public **header** and **Explore / footer** menus. Desk path: **The Website → Public site navigation** (visible to Administrator, Editor, and Developer roles only).

| Field | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Header links** (`primaryLinks`) | Top bar (always visible) |
| **Explore links** (`secondaryLinks`) | Homepage Explore, collection sidebars, footer |

Each item is a **Navigation link**: public **Label**, site **Path** (starts with `/`), **Kind** (`page` or `search`), and optional Studio-only **Purpose**. Search is a kind of menu item so editors can remove or reorder it; the website renders it as the header search field, not a text link. Paths must be unique across both lists.

Do not create a second navigation document. Duplicate and delete are disabled so the ID `siteNavigation` stays stable. Related: [Edit public site navigation](../common-tasks/edit-site-navigation.mdx).

## Research Articles & Overviews

Use a **Research Article** for long-form modern research, overviews, or interactive pages—not for raw transcriptions of historical clippings.

Key fields:

- **Archive ID** (`archiveId`) — unique catalog number when matching an imported Book or legacy record
- **Page title** and required **URL slug** (generated from the title)
- **Show in the public highlight grid** (`featuredOnSite`) and optional **Highlight order** (`featuredRank`, 1–24) — home research teasers. Rank is order, not quality; leave off unless you are curating the public site.
- **Townships** — geographic scope of the research article
- **People Mentioned** (`peopleMentioned`) — historical persons discussed in the article
- **Organizations** (`organizations`) — historical businesses and organizations discussed in the article (one field; pick either document type)
- **Page content & layout canvas** — Portable Text with:
  - **Historical Images** — links to cataloged photographs in The Archive → Historical Images (optional caption/alt override; semantic `imageRole`: figure / aside). Prefer this when the photograph is already cataloged.
  - **Uploaded Images** — caption, alt, and semantic `imageRole`. Use only if the photograph is not in Historical Images. If it belongs in the archive, catalog it there first, then insert **Historical Image**.
  - **Interactive map modules** (`mapYear`, embedded `mapUrl`)
  - **Nested navigation portals** linking to other research articles
- **Notes & References** (`notesAndReferences`) — published bibliographic notes, endnotes, and web links (rich text with URL marks). Appears with the article on the public site.
- **Internal Comments** (`internalComments`) — Studio-only working notes. **Will not appear on the public site.**

Related: [Publish a research article](../common-tasks/publish-research-article.mdx), [Set Archive IDs](../common-tasks/set-archive-ids.mdx)

## Then & Now

Use **Then & Now** for a Quarterly landmark comparison: a historical photograph and a modern photograph of the same place, each with Portable Text commentary. One document is the source of truth for the issue table of contents and for a standalone public page. Do not use Research Article or TEHS Quarterly Article for this format.

Key fields:

- **Page title** and required **URL slug** (generated from the title)
- **Show in the public highlight grid** (`featuredOnSite`) and optional **Highlight order** (`featuredRank`, 1–24) — home teasers. Rank is order, not quality; leave off unless you are curating the public site.
- **Issue** (`issueRef`) — required reference to the printed TEHS Quarterly issue
- **Author Name** (`authorText`), **Start Page**, and optional **End Page** — publication metadata for the issue index
- **Introduction** — optional opening Portable Text that is not tied to either photograph
- **Then** and **Now** (`thenAndNowView`) — each view has:
  - **Photograph source** (`historicalImage` or `upload`). Prefer a cataloged Historical Image. Upload only if the photograph is not in that collection (typical for a contemporary “now” photo).
  - Optional caption and alt override (catalog caption is the fallback for Historical Images)
  - Optional **Year Taken** on uploads (cataloged images use **Date Taken** on the Historical Image)
  - **Commentary** — Portable Text for that photograph
- **Townships**, **People Mentioned**, **Properties / Historic Sites Mentioned**, and **Organizations**
- **Notes & References** (`notesAndReferences`) — published bibliographic notes on the piece
- **Internal Comments** (`internalComments`) — Studio-only working notes. **Will not appear on the public site.**

Desk path: **The Website → Then & Now**. From an issue, use **Then & Now in this issue**.

Related: [Publish a Then & Now](../common-tasks/publish-then-and-now.mdx)

## TEHS Quarterly Issues

A **TEHS Quarterly Issue** is one printed number of the journal (volume and issue number). Cover image, issue PDF, and publication date live here so they are not copied onto every article.

Key fields:

- **Volume** and **Issue / Number** — unique together (e.g. Volume 22, Number 1)
- **Combined / double issue** — check only when one physical magazine covers two numbers (e.g. Vol. 44, Numbers 1 and 2). **Also numbered as** appears when the checkbox is on; it is the higher number, not a page range.
- **Season** — Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter when the issue is dated by season (Fall is stored as Autumn). Do not invent a month from the season.
- **Publication Date** — structured historical date: month and year when named (January 1984), year only when dated by season
- **Source Key** — optional archive stem such as `v22n1` or `v44n1+2` (set by the catalog import)
- **Cover Image** and **Issue PDF** — optional; HTML-imported volumes often have neither
- **Table of Contents Notes** — optional editorial blurb (errata, supplemental web notes). Do not store a duplicate TOC; the website generates the contents list from articles in this issue.

Desk path: **The Website → TEHS Quarterly Issues**. Open an issue to edit it, to list **Articles in this issue** (ordered by start page), or to list **Then & Now in this issue**.

Catalog import (issues first, then article stubs without bodies):

```bash
bun run csv-import:quarterly -- --issues
bun run csv-import:quarterly -- --article-stubs
```

HTML article bodies are a later pass: `bun run csv-import:quarterly -- --volume 22`.

## TEHS Quarterly Articles

A **TEHS Quarterly Article** stores the full digitized text of a Quarterly piece, plus a reference to its issue and entity links.

Key fields:

- **Publication Details** — title, author name, **Issue** (required reference to a TEHS Quarterly Issue), start page, optional end page, and source key/URL
- **Summary / Abstract** — optional short abstract for search and indexes
- **Article Content** — Portable Text body with:
  - Standard text blocks (paragraphs, headings)
  - **Historical Images** — links to cataloged photographs in The Archive → Historical Images (optional caption/alt override; semantic `imageRole`). Prefer this when the photograph is already cataloged.
  - Inline **Uploaded Images** (caption and alt text). Use only if the photograph is not in Historical Images. If it belongs in the archive, catalog it there first, then insert **Historical Image**.
  - **Original Print Page Break** blocks to preserve academic pagination (e.g., "Page 3") without disrupting reading flow
  - **Notes & References** (`notesAndReferences`) — published bibliographic notes, endnotes, and web links (rich text with URL marks). Appears with the article on the public site.
- **Tagged Entities** — **Subjects** (subject categories such as Railroads or Genealogy), plus references to properties, people, and organizations (including businesses) mentioned in the article
- **Internal Comments** (`internalComments`) — Studio-only working notes. **Will not appear on the public site.**

Author bylines stay as **Author Name** (`authorText`). Do not use Historical Person documents for Quarterly writers unless that person is also cataloged as a historical figure mentioned in the archive.

Archive documents (primary sources and historical images) link these under **Research References** (`citations`). That field is a document reference, not a pasted URL: many archive items can share one article. It also accepts **Then & Now** documents. Historical images also have a separate **References** rich-text field for books, PDFs, and other links that are not Quarterly articles or Then & Now pieces in this Studio.

**Notes & References** on a Research Article, Then & Now, or Quarterly Article is bibliography *on the article itself*. It is not the same as archive **Research References** (document links *to* a Quarterly article or Then & Now) or image **References** (books, PDFs, and other URLs on a Historical Image).

### Dynamic table of contents

Because issues, articles, and Then & Now pieces are structured data rather than static HTML, the website can generate volume indexes on the fly. For example, to recreate the Volume 15 table of contents:

```groq
*[_type == "quarterlyIssue" && volume == 15] | order(issueNumber asc) {
  volume,
  issueNumber,
  publicationDate,
  "articles": *[_type in ["quarterlyArticle", "thenAndNow"] && issueRef._ref == ^._id] | order(startPage asc) {
    _type,
    title,
    authorText,
    startPage,
    endPage
  }
}
```
