# Taxonomies & Entities

Shared records that organize and connect archive and website content. Create these once and reference them from many documents.

## Counties

County-level geography (e.g. Chester, Delaware, Montgomery). Each county **name must be unique**. Create counties under **Taxonomies & Entities → Counties** before assigning them to townships.

## Townships

Municipality-level geography (e.g. Tredyffrin, Easttown). Each township **must** belong to a parent county.

## Locations

Villages or named places (e.g. Paoli, Berwyn, Cedar Hollow). Each location **must** belong to a parent township.

## Historical Persons

People mentioned in sources, depicted in historical images, or tied to properties, businesses, and organizations: name parts, born/died, alternate spellings, census/occupation records. Tag people to broader **Family Lineages**, and log known **Immediate Relatives** (spouse, parent, child, sibling, and so on) on the person document. Photograph tags live on the **Historical Image** (`people`); each person shows a reverse **Historical Images** list of photographs they are tagged in.

## Families / Lineages

Broader family groups (e.g. The Bean Family, The Havard Family): family name and optional historical background. People are tagged to a lineage from the **person** document (`familyLines`), not the reverse. Use **Known Immediate Relatives** on each person for specific person-to-person relationships.

## Properties & Buildings

Physical sites classified by **Property Type** (dwelling, estate/farm, church, inn/tavern, industrial, institutional, railroad/station): historical name, optional parent estate, location/township, residents, modern address, year built, and an ordered **Title Chain** of Deed / Land Instrument documents. Link occupying businesses or organizations from the **business** or **organization** document (`associatedProperties`), not from the property. Valley Forge association is via location, not a property type.

**Property Type** is building classification for the entity (one required type). It is not a Subject Category—categories like Churches or Railroads are archive search themes for clippings and photos.

## Deeds & Land Instruments

One conveyance or land instrument in a chain of title (patent, deed, will, probate, warrant, survey, tax return, atlas, or other): parties (person refs and/or free text), textual and optional exact dates, area, cost text plus optional normalized amount/currency/kind, book/page reference, associated properties, branch label, notes, and optional scan or Primary Source transcription link. Create these under **Taxonomies & Entities → Deeds & Land Instruments**, then order them on a property’s **Title Chain**. Do not model chain-of-title rows as Primary Sources.

## Businesses

Named historical commercial and industrial firms—stores, mills, lumber yards, water companies, silica/ore firms—with description, years active, owners (people), and associated property/site references.

Desk path: **Taxonomies & Entities → Businesses**. There is no commercial subtype field; being a Business document is the classification.

Businesses can carry a **Migration Mapping Key** so the CSV import links archive documents to the correct firm.

## Organizations

Named historical civic, community, and institutional groups—congregations, fire companies, libraries, horse shows, schools as operators—with description, years active, leaders / associated people, and associated property/site references.

Desk path: **Taxonomies & Entities → Organizations**. All of these records share one flat list; there is no civic vs institutional subtype.

Subject Categories such as Businesses or Organizations are separate—they tag archive material for search, not Business or Organization documents.

Organizations can carry a **Migration Mapping Key** (e.g. `Lincoln`) so the CSV import links archive documents to the correct group. Primary sources, historical images, and research articles reference both businesses and organizations via an **Organizations** field—distinct from **Subjects** (theme categories).

## Subject Categories

Broad **themes for archive search** (e.g. Schools, Railroads, Farms, Genealogy). Tag primary sources and historical images so related material can be filtered. Do not use categories to classify a Property, Business, or Organization—use Property Type on properties, and create a Business or Organization document for named entities. Categories and townships also have Migration Mapping Keys (visible during migration) for CSV keyword matching.

## Donation Categories

**Material types for gifts** (e.g. Photographic prints, Digital photographs, Newspaper clipping, Postcards, Slides, Drawings, Posters). Tag **Donation** documents so mixed gifts can list each material present. A donation can reference several categories.

Donation Categories are not Subject Categories: they classify what kind of material was donated, not archive search themes like Schools or Railroads. Create them under **Taxonomies & Entities → Donation Categories**, or use **Create new** when linking from a Donation form.
