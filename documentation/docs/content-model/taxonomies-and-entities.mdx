# Taxonomies & Entities

Shared records that organize and connect archive and website content. Create these once and reference them from many documents.

## Townships

Municipality-level geography (e.g. Tredyffrin, Easttown).

## Locations

Villages or named places (e.g. Paoli, Berwyn, Cedar Hollow). Each location **must** belong to a parent township.

## Historical Persons

People mentioned in sources or tied to properties and organizations: name parts, born/died, alternate spellings, census/occupation records. Tag people to broader **Family Lineages**, and log known **Immediate Relatives** (spouse, parent, child, sibling, and so on) on the person document.

## Families / Lineages

Broader family groups (e.g. The Bean Family, The Havard Family): family name and optional historical background. People are tagged to a lineage from the **person** document (`familyLines`), not the reverse. Use **Known Immediate Relatives** on each person for specific person-to-person relationships.

## Properties & Buildings

Physical sites classified by **Property Type** (dwelling, estate/farm, church, inn/tavern, industrial, institutional, railroad/station): historical name, optional parent estate, location/township, residents, modern address, year built, and an ordered **Title Chain** of Deed / Land Instrument documents. Legacy free-text Chester County deed citations remain only for migration. Link occupying organizations from the **organization** document (`associatedProperties`), not from the property. Valley Forge association is via location, not a property type.

**Property Type** is building classification for the entity (one required type). It is not a Subject Category—categories like Churches or Railroads are archive search themes for clippings and photos.

## Deeds & Land Instruments

One conveyance or land instrument in a chain of title (patent, deed, will, probate, warrant, survey, tax return, atlas, or other): parties (person refs and/or free text), textual and optional exact dates, area, cost text plus optional normalized amount/currency/kind, book/page reference, associated properties, branch label, notes, and optional scan or Primary Source transcription link. Create these under **Taxonomies & Entities → Deeds & Land Instruments**, then order them on a property’s **Title Chain**. Do not model chain-of-title rows as Primary Sources.

## Organizations

Named historical organizations—commercial businesses, civic groups, and institutions—with optional **Organization type** (civic, commercial, institutional), description, years active, owners (people), and associated property/site references.

In the Studio desk, **Organizations** is split into filtered lists (Commercial / Industrial, Civic / Community, Institutional) plus **All organizations**. Creating from a typed list presets Organization type. One document type covers all three kinds.

**Organization type** classifies the entity. Subject Categories such as Businesses or Organizations are separate—they tag archive material for search, not organization documents.

Organizations can carry a **Migration Mapping Key** (e.g. `Lincoln`) so the CSV import links archive documents to the correct organization. Primary sources, historical images, and research articles reference organizations via an **Organizations** field—distinct from **Subjects** (theme categories).

## Subject Categories

Broad **themes for archive search** (e.g. Schools, Railroads, Farms, Genealogy). Tag primary sources and historical images so related material can be filtered. Do not use categories to classify a Property or Organization—use Property Type and Organization type on those documents instead. Categories and townships also have Migration Mapping Keys (visible during migration) for CSV keyword matching.

## Donation Categories

**Material types for gifts** (e.g. Photographic prints, Digital photographs, Newspaper clipping, Postcards, Slides, Drawings, Posters). Tag **Donation** documents so mixed gifts can list each material present. A donation can reference several categories.

Donation Categories are not Subject Categories: they classify what kind of material was donated, not archive search themes like Schools or Railroads. Create them under **Taxonomies & Entities → Donation Categories**, or use **Create new** when linking from a Donation form.
