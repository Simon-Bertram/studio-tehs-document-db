# Taxonomies & Entities

Shared records that organize and connect archive and website content. Create these once and reference them from many documents.

## Townships

Municipality-level geography (e.g. Tredyffrin, Easttown).

## Locations

Villages or named places (e.g. Paoli, Berwyn, Cedar Hollow). Each location **must** belong to a parent township.

## Historical Persons

People mentioned in sources or tied to properties and businesses: name parts, born/died, alternate spellings, census/occupation records. Tag people to broader **Family Lineages**, and log known **Immediate Relatives** (spouse, parent, child, sibling, and so on) on the person document.

## Families / Lineages

Broader family groups (e.g. The Bean Family, The Havard Family): family name and optional historical background. People are tagged to a lineage from the **person** document (`familyLines`), not the reverse. Use **Known Immediate Relatives** on each person for specific person-to-person relationships.

## Properties & Buildings

Physical sites classified by type (dwelling, estate/farm, church, inn/tavern, industrial, institutional, railroad/station): historical name, optional parent estate, location/township, related businesses/organisations, residents, modern address, year built, Chester County deed citations. Valley Forge association is via location, not a property type.

## Businesses

Named historical businesses and organisations with optional business type (civic, commercial, institutional), description, years active, owners (people), and associated property/site references.

## Subject Categories

Broad themes for discovery (e.g. Schools, Railroads, Farms). Tag primary sources and historical images so related material can be filtered.
