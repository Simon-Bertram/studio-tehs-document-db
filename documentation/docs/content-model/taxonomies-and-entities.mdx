# Taxonomies & Entities

Shared records that organize and connect archive and website content. Create these once and reference them from many documents.

## Townships

Municipality-level geography (e.g. Tredyffrin, Easttown).

## Locations

Villages or named places (e.g. Paoli, Berwyn, Cedar Hollow). Each location **must** belong to a parent township.

## Historical Persons

People mentioned in sources or tied to properties and businesses: name parts, born/died, alternate spellings, census/occupation records. Tag people to broader **Family Lineages**, and log known **Immediate Relatives** (spouse, parent, child, sibling, and so on) on the person document.

## Families / Lineages

Broader family groups (e.g. The Bean Family, The Havard Family): family name and optional historical background. People are tagged to a lineage from the **person** document (`familyLines`), not the reverse. Use **Known Immediate Relatives** on each person for specific person-to-person relationships.

## Properties & Buildings

Physical sites classified by **Property Type** (dwelling, estate/farm, church, inn/tavern, industrial, institutional, railroad/station): historical name, optional parent estate, location/township, residents, modern address, year built, Chester County deed citations. Link occupying organisations from the **business** document (`locations`), not from the property. Valley Forge association is via location, not a property type.

**Property Type** is building classification for the entity (one required type). It is not a Subject Category—categories like Churches or Railroads are archive search themes for clippings and photos.

## Businesses

Named historical businesses and organisations with optional **Business Type** (civic, commercial, institutional), description, years active, owners (people), and associated property/site references.

**Business Type** classifies the organisation. Subject Categories such as Businesses or Organizations are separate—they tag archive material for search, not business documents.

## Subject Categories

Broad **themes for archive search** (e.g. Schools, Railroads, Farms, Genealogy). Tag primary sources and historical images so related material can be filtered. Do not use categories to classify a Property or Business—use Property Type and Business Type on those documents instead.
