# The Archive

The Archive holds primary historical media: transcribed clippings and letters, cataloged photographs, and donation / accession records. Prefer creating reusable people, places, and categories under Taxonomies & Entities, then link them here.

## Primary Sources / Transcriptions

Use a **Primary Source** for a single historical piece of media (newspaper ad, letter, notice)—not for modern essays.

Key field groups:

- **Identity** — `archiveId` (unique Archive ID), title, textual and/or exact dates, publication name
- **Content** — scan image, full transcription, sheriff’s sale flag and legal writ
- **Place** — associated properties (preferred) or standalone township; people mentioned
- **Research** — subject categories (archive search themes) and Quarterly citations

Related: [Add a primary source](../common-tasks/add-primary-source.mdx), [Enter a sheriff’s sale](../common-tasks/enter-sheriff-sale.mdx)

## Historical Images

Use a **Historical Image** for photographs with provenance and rights metadata.

Key field groups:

- **Identity** — `archiveId` (unique Archive ID), serial number, caption, date taken
- **Content** — required photograph file, full description
- **Place** — specific location (preferred) or township
- **Provenance / Rights** — source, contributor, linked donation, photographer, rights, archivist notes
- **Research** — subjects (archive search themes) and Quarterly citations

Link **Donation** under Provenance / Rights when the photograph belongs to a recorded gift. If the image is part of a new donation, create the Donation document first (or use Create new on the field), then link it.

Related: [Catalog a historical image](../common-tasks/catalog-historical-image.mdx), [Record a donation](../common-tasks/record-a-donation.mdx)

## Donations

Use a **Donation** for an accession or gift record—what was given, by whom, and when—not for cataloging an individual photograph or transcription. Historical Images can reference a Donation under Provenance / Rights.

Key fields:

- **Donation Name**, **Donation ID**, **Donation Acquisition Date**, **Donation Description**, **Donor**
- **Donation Categories** — one or more material types (e.g. Photographic prints, Postcards, Slides)

Desk path: **The Archive → Donations**. Material types themselves live under **Taxonomies & Entities → Donation Categories**.

Related: [Record a donation](../common-tasks/record-a-donation.mdx)
