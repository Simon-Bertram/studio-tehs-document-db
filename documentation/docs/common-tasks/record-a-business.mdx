# Record a business or organisation

## Goal

Capture a historical business or organisation (company, civic group, institution) with type, owners, years active, and the property sites it occupied.

## Property vs business

- Create a [property](./model-a-property.mdx) for the physical site first when the building or tract is known.
- The business document is the *organisation or company*, not the building.
- Do **not** invent a property type for “organisation”—classify the site on the property (e.g. Institutional for a library or fire hall), then link both ways if useful.

## Steps

1. Open **Taxonomies & Entities → Businesses** and create a new document.
2. Set **Business Name** (required).
3. Optionally set **Business Type**:
   - **Civic / Community** — fire company, library, conservancy, horse show
   - **Commercial / Industrial** — mill operators, water companies, silica/ore firms
   - **Institutional** — Lincoln Institution, military units as operators
4. Optionally add **Description** and **Years Active** (freeform, e.g. `1870–1920`).
5. Link **Owners / Operators** to Historical Person documents.
6. Under **Associated Properties / Sites**, reference one or more **Properties** (not villages—put geography on the property).
7. On the property document, you may also set **Related Businesses / Organisations** so the link is visible from the site.
8. Publish when ready.

## Field notes

- Create person and property entities first if they do not already exist.
- Places of business are property references only.
