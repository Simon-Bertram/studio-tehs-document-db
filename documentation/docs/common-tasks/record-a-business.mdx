# Record a business

## Goal

Capture a historical commercial or industrial business—mill operators, stores, lumber yards, water companies, silica/ore firms—with owners, years active, and the property sites it occupied.

## Property vs business

- Create a [property](./model-a-property.mdx) for the physical site first when the building or tract is known.
- The business document is the *company or firm*, not the building.
- Do **not** invent a property type for “business”—classify the site on the property (e.g. Industrial for a mill), then link the property from this business under **Associated Properties / Sites**.
- Civic groups, congregations, and institutions belong on an [organization](./record-an-organization.mdx) document, not here.

## Business vs Subject Categories

- **Business** = this named commercial entity under Taxonomies & Entities.
- **Subject Categories** such as Businesses = themes for [tagging archive clippings and photos](./tag-with-categories.mdx). They are not set on business documents.
- Use this document for the named firm; use categories only when cataloging primary sources or historical images.

## Steps

1. Open **Taxonomies & Entities → Businesses** and create a new document.
2. Set **Business Name** (required).
3. Optionally set **Migration Mapping Key** to the legacy CSV keyword. This field is visible during migration so you can fill keys; hide it after cutover.
4. Optionally add **Description** and **Active From / Active To** (often year-only).
5. Link **Owners / Operators** to Historical Person documents.
6. Under **Associated Properties / Sites**, reference one or more **Properties** (not villages—put geography on the property). This is the single source of truth for business↔site links.
7. Publish when ready.

## Field notes

- Create person and property entities first if they do not already exist.
- Places of business are property references only.
- **Migration Mapping Key** maps CSV keywords onto this business during import. Entity keywords belong here, not on a Subject Category.
